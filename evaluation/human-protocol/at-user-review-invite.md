# Invitation template: paid review by a daily assistive-technology user

**Why this matters.** The single biggest gap in this case study's evidence is
that no one who uses assistive technology daily has reviewed the module. Every
other method (automated tools, scripted keyboard tests, PDF inspection) is a
proxy. This review is the strongest evidence available and should be **paid
work**, not a favor. (The project owner sends this; templates and outreach are
not AI-substitutable.)

---

Subject: Paid review opportunity — accessibility review of an open course module (1–2 hours)

Hi [Name],

I'm [owner name], and I built an accessibility remediation case study: a real,
openly licensed university course unit (MIT OpenCourseWare, CC BY-NC-SA)
audited against WCAG 2.1 AA and rebuilt as accessible HTML
(https://iwyusdaz.github.io/accessibility-remediation-case-study/).

Automated and scripted testing are done, and a screen-reader protocol exists —
but you are the missing expert. I would like to pay you for 1–2 hours to use
the module the way you actually do, and tell me where it fights you.

**What you'd do**
- Use the remediated module (lesson, slides, video page, transcript, quiz)
  with your own assistive technology setup (any platform/browser combination
  you prefer).
- Note anything confusing, slow, mislabeled, or broken — small annoyances
  included.
- A short follow-up conversation (~20 minutes) about what the fixes missed.

**Logistics**
- Rate: [$X/hour], paid within [N] days; estimated 1–2 hours, you decide when.
- With your permission, I'll quote short attributed statements in the case
  study's evidence section; otherwise your notes are anonymized.
- You keep full control: skip any task, stop any time, no personal data
  collected beyond what you choose to share.

**What you'd get**
- Payment, credit (if you want it), and the satisfaction of catching what
  automated tools structurally cannot.

If you're interested, reply and I'll send the task list and the consent summary.

Thanks,
[Owner name] · [Email] · [LinkedIn]

## Consent summary (send with the task list)

1. Participation is paid and optional; you may stop at any time.
2. We collect only: your feedback notes, the assistive technology you used,

3. Quotes appear only with your explicit written permission.
4. You may review any quote before publication.
5. Results will be published in the repository under `evaluation/human-protocol/results/`.
